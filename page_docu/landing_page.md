# LandingPage — `/`

## 1. What It Is Doing

Editorial home page (~840 lines). It is the user's first impression of MarketDNA. The page explains what the platform does, showcases all modules, and articulates the research philosophy.

**Important**: LandingPage uses a **completely separate editorial palette** (`W` object with sage/moss/warm tones) and IBM Plex Serif as the primary font — NOT the main `usePalette()`/`useTokens()` design system. This is intentional for editorial differentiation. Do not port this page to the main design system.

**Page sections** (top to bottom):
1. **Hero** — dual CTA: "Start Researching" + "Explore Modules". Tag pill, headline, subheadline, mini-stats strip.
2. **Intelligence strip** — 3 live intelligence tiles (mocked: Market DNA score, Breadth, India VIX).
3. **Featured module** — Stock DNA module with mock DNA score ring.
4. **Topics row** — 4 research topic chips (Macro, Options, Portfolio, Quant).
5. **8-module grid** — cards for all 8 research modules with description and CTA.
6. **Platform stats** (dark bg) — 5 years data, 48 stocks, 100+ indicators, etc.
7. **4 AI agents** — same 4 as AgentsPage, editorial layout.
8. **Research philosophy** — 4 principles with icons.
9. **Pipeline** — 4-step data pipeline: Ingest → Feature → Validate → Insight.
10. **Tech strip** — Python, DuckDB, Polars, FastAPI, React logos.
11. **CTA** — "Begin your research session."
12. **Footer** — links, copyright.

`PlatformCard` shows mocked market DNA scores and index prices — these are static placeholders.

## 2. Optimization

- `PlatformCard` shows mocked data. Wire it to `/api/market-dna/current` and `/api/breadth/latest` for live values. A live market health strip on the home page immediately communicates platform value.
- Intelligence strip (Market DNA, Breadth, India VIX) tiles are hardcoded mock values — same fix as above.
- The 8-module grid cards are statically defined. When new modules are added, this list must be manually updated. Consider fetching module metadata from an API registry.
- Hero subheadline is generic. It should adapt based on current market regime: if regime is bearish, show "Navigate bear markets with data-driven research" rather than a generic pitch.
- IBM Plex Serif is imported but only used on LandingPage — this adds ~80KB to the bundle. Consider lazy-loading it only when the root route is active.

---

## 3. Lessons Learnt

- The editorial palette (`W` object) is intentionally separate from `usePalette()`. Do not attempt to unify them. The Landing page intentionally feels like a research publication; the app feels like an analytics terminal. They serve different contexts.
- Static mock values on a public-facing page erode trust. If a user visits after login and sees "Market DNA: 72" that is the same every day, they will notice immediately. Live data builds trust; static data destroys it.
- The research philosophy section ("Research over opinions. Evidence over narratives.") is one of the strongest parts of the page. It should be prominently linked from every page's footer to reinforce the product's positioning.

## 6. Suggestions to Achieve the Objective

1. **Live market context strip**: the top of the landing page should show today's Market DNA score, Breadth score, and India VIX in real-time. Users should immediately understand market health before navigating anywhere. This is the single highest-value change for a research platform.
2. **Research brief section**: a daily auto-generated 3-bullet brief ("Today's market is trending with Breadth at 68. Three stocks in the NIFTY 50 are showing Accumulation signals. HDFC Bank is at a 52-week RS high.") — generated by the AI Research Assistant from live Feature Store data.
3. **Portfolio performance snapshot**: for returning users with a saved portfolio, show 1-line portfolio attribution on the landing page. Turns the page from a brochure into a live dashboard entry point.
4. **Module discovery quiz**: "What are you researching?" → user selects Options / Portfolio / Macro → page recommends the 2 most relevant modules. Reduces time-to-value for new users.
5. **Replace mock PlatformCard with live RegimeDNA**: fetch current regime scores for BANKNIFTY, NIFTY IT, NIFTY AUTO and show them as live mini-cards on the landing page. Real data immediately demonstrates the platform's capabilities better than any marketing copy.
